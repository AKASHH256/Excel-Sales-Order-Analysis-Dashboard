# Excel Sales & Order Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze sales and order data and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard combines data cleaning, calculated metrics, Excel formulas, Pivot Tables, and visualizations to analyze customer behavior, product performance, sales trends, delivery performance, discounts, and city-wise results.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance
- Identify top customers based on sales
- Identify the most frequently ordered products
- Compare sales performance across cities
- Analyze monthly sales and order trends
- Understand gender-wise sales patterns
- Evaluate the impact of discounts and delivery charges
- Analyze coupon usage
- Track delivery performance

## 📌 Key Dashboard Metrics

- Total Unique Customers
- Total Orders Placed
- Total Unique Products
- Average Customer Age
- Average Delivery Days
- Orders with ₹0 Delivery Charge
- Total Amount Generated
- Total Discount Offered
- Total Amount After Discount
- Total Amount After Discount & Delivery Charge
